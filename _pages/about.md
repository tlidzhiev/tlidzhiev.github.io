## Bio

Hi, I'm Timur! I'm currently a Master's student in the [M2 in Artificial Intelligence](https://ensimag.grenoble-inp.fr/fr/formation/master-of-artificial-intelligence) at [Université Grenoble Alpes](https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/en). My research centers on physics-informed machine learning, applied mathematics, and representation learning, especially spectral and geometric deep learning. More recently, I've been digging into diffusion models and flow matching, and how these ideas might apply to physics and bioinformatics.

**I'm looking for PhD opportunities for 2027.**

---

I received my BSc in Computer Science from [HSE's Faculty of Computer Science](https://cs.hse.ru/en/) in 2025 and am also continuing there for a Master's degree. I've been a senior teaching assistant at HSE for Real Analysis, Numerical Analysis, and Recommender Systems.

Previously, I was an ML Engineer on the R&D GeoML team at VTB Bank, one of Russia's top-3 banks, working on representation learning for geospatial data and for sequences of bank transactions.

If you'd like to talk research, collaboration, or anything in this space, feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:trlidzhiev@gmail.com).
