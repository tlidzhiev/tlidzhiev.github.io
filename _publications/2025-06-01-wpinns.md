Trains physics-informed neural networks on a weak, integral form of the mass-conservation law instead of the usual differential form, avoiding derivative estimates near sharp discontinuities such as saturation fronts. Improves stability and mass-conservation accuracy for multiphase flow in porous media — relevant to applications like enhanced oil recovery and groundwater management.
