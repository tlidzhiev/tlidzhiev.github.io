Learns spectral-style feature coordinates for downstream tasks by optimizing a modified Dirichlet-energy objective with a learnable, data-dependent metric, in place of a fixed kernel or graph Laplacian. The resulting coordinates are shaped by the task itself, rather than being generic eigenfunctions of a fixed operator.
